Surf Finder — Privacy notice

Version 2026-10-04

Surf Finder is a personal, non-commercial project. When you create an account we store:

  • Your profile: email address, name, profile picture and language — from Google, Microsoft or GitHub if you sign in with them, or what you type when you sign up by email. Email passwords are stored only as a salted hash.
  • Sign-up and visit details: when you signed up and last signed in, how many times, your IP address, approximate country, browser, operating system and device type.
  • How you use the app: filters you apply and spots you open.
  • Your choices: when you accepted this notice, and whether you want the occasional newsletter.

Why: to confirm you're a real person, keep the app safe from abuse, and understand which spots and filters are useful. We never sell or share your data, and never post anything to your Google, Microsoft or GitHub account.

Your data stays on the owner's computer until you ask for it to be deleted. Accounts whose email is never confirmed are deleted automatically after 7 days. Sign-up and "resend email" attempts are logged with their IP address for one day, only to limit abuse. To see or delete your data, or to stop the newsletter, reply to the confirmation email.
